Beckon Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Beckon lets you control your phone with hand gestures, using the front camera.

Camera

The app uses your front camera to detect your hand. Camera images are processed on your device in memory, in real time. They are never saved, recorded, uploaded or shared.

Accessibility Service

With your permission, the app uses Android's Accessibility Service to perform taps, swipes and long presses when you make gestures, to detect which app is in front, and to read the buttons currently on screen so it can tap the nearest one. It does not read passwords. It does not store or send screen content. You can turn it off at any time in Settings > Accessibility, or withdraw consent in the app's Settings.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your settings, which apps have gestures enabled, your consent choice and the time of an ad unlock, only on your device. We do not run servers and do not collect accounts, names, emails, contacts, location or messages.

Ads

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device identifiers (for example the advertising ID), IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Where required, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

You can revoke the camera permission and the accessibility service at any time in Android settings, withdraw consent in the app, or uninstall the app.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above.
